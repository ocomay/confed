# Spectrum Axes: Resources & Economy

**Summary**: Detailed comparison of 2 axes from [[confederation-spectrum-framework]] — water resource allocation and economic integration — across the five reference configurations.

**Sources**: See [[confederation-spectrum-framework]] for the full sourcing note and reliability caveats applying to all five configuration columns.

**Last updated**: 2026-09-03

---

## Water Resource Allocation

| Configuration | Description |
|---|---|
| **1. Status Quo** | Documented ~80/20 Israeli/Palestinian allocation split of the Mountain Aquifer despite 80-90% of its recharge area lying in Palestinian territory (see [[final-status-core-disputes]]) |
| **2. Full Annexation** | Consolidates full Israeli control over West Bank water resources including Area C aquifers |
| **3. State of All Its Citizens** | Presumably unified national water management for the whole state and population |
| **4. A Land for All** | Mentioned only as an example of "interdependence" (shared aquifers) in general terms — no specific allocation formula found in platform text reviewed |
| **5. Two States for Two Peoples** | Typically addressed via a joint water committee/shared-management mechanism (continuing and reforming the Oslo II water provisions), with a negotiated proportional allocation — more institutionally specific on this axis than ALFA's current documented platform |

## Economic Integration

| Configuration | Description |
|---|---|
| **1. Status Quo** | Palestinian economy structurally subordinate to Israel's via the Oslo-era Paris Protocol customs union, combined with severe Israeli-imposed movement/access restrictions limiting Palestinian economic development |
| **2. Full Annexation** | Full absorption into the Israeli economic/customs system without accompanying full civil/political rights — dependency formalized rather than resolved |
| **3. State of All Its Citizens** | Fully unified single national economy |
| **4. A Land for All** | Explicit aspiration toward shared economic institutions and an integrated labor market, without a detailed equalization mechanism in the platform text reviewed (see the ~30x income gap critique in [[confederation-model-critique]]) |
| **5. Two States for Two Peoples** | Separate sovereign economies with a negotiated trade/customs relationship, likely replacing or reforming the Paris Protocol framework — economically more separative than ALFA's shared-institutions aspiration |

## Key Cross-Axis Observation

**A Land for All is the least institutionally specific of the five configurations on both resource axes** — the platform text reviewed offers only general "interdependence" language for water and no detailed equalization mechanism for economic integration, in contrast to its notably precise commitments on Jerusalem governance and citizenship law (see [[axes-territory-borders-security]] and [[axes-citizenship-rights]]). This is a real gap given the ~30x income disparity and the ~80/20 water allocation asymmetry are both concrete, quantifiable problems the platform would need to address for the "shared institutions" premise to be credible — see [[confederation-model-critique]] for how critics (from both the Palestinian-rights and realist directions) treat this economic asymmetry as the single most convergent point of criticism.

By contrast, **the classic two-state model is more institutionally developed on water specifically** (building on the existing Oslo II joint water committee framework) even though it is generally the more separative model on most other axes.
